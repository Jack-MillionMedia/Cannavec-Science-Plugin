Cannavec Science API

Research-grade cannabis-science briefs — primary-source anchored, GRADE-honest. Phase 1 (curated, offline) endpoints:

  • GET /api/health — liveness & capability probe
  • GET /api/answer?question=... — cited research brief (JSON; add &format=markdown for prose; add &augment=true to weave in live findings)
  • POST /api/answer — body {"question":"..."}
  • GET /api/discover?query=... — live PubMed / ClinicalTrials.gov (+ opt-in ChEMBL / Europe PMC) fan-out with a cross-source synthesis verdict (Phase 2)
  • GET /api/rigor?text=... — phytochemistry & banned-pattern audit
  • GET /api/registries — curated-registry inventory

Examples:

curl "/api/answer?question=CBD evidence in Dravet syndrome&format=markdown"
curl "/api/discover?query=cannabis melatonin circadian&sources=pubmed,ctgov"

Live discovery (/api/discover and ?augment=true) authenticates to NCBI via the NCBI_API_KEY environment variable. The curated /api/answer path needs no key and works offline.
